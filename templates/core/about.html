Our purpose

Indian craft and the people who make it.

Virasat is a marketplace for Indian craft. It connects buyers with named artisans, explains the tradition behind each piece, and keeps the maker visible from listing to delivery.

What guides us

Three things we protect.

The maker stays visible

Every published product belongs to a named artisan with a profile, a place and a craft speciality. Verification is shown where it applies.

Craft comes with context

Listings describe materials, technique, care and heritage. Product pages link back to the craft tradition and the region it comes from.

Fair pay is recorded

Order records separate the artisan amount from the platform fee, and the public impact page reports what artisans earned on delivered work.

How listings are written

Clear language, with the artisan in control.

Artisans can begin with a few notes about a piece. Virasat can draft the longer description, but the artisan reviews and edits it before publishing. If no AI key is configured, the same flow uses a local writing fallback, so the marketplace still works.

Join as an artisan
  • No invisible automation. AI-assisted drafts are labelled until the artisan edits them.
  • No forced dependency. The catalog, cart and orders work without an AI key.
  • No lost context. Craft stories, materials and maker details stay attached to the piece.
